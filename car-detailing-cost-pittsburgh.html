Car detailing prices in Pittsburgh range from $50 for a basic exterior wash to $500+ for a full detail package with deep interior cleaning, paint protection, and add-on services. The variance comes down to what's actually included, who's doing the work, and the type of vehicle. Here's a full breakdown so you know what you're paying for.
What Affects Price

Key Pricing Factors

Basic wash vs. full detail
A car wash is $10-25 and lasts a day. A proper exterior detail is $80-200 and includes clay bar, paint sealant, and tire dressing. A full detail covers interior and exterior and runs $175-$400+.
Mobile vs. shop
Mobile detailing prices are usually similar to shop pricing because you're paying for time and product, not real estate. The convenience of having someone come to you is the value-add.
Vehicle size
Sedans are baseline. SUVs add 20-40%. Pickup trucks and large SUVs (Suburban, Expedition, Yukon XL) add 50-100% because they take longer to do properly.
Condition
A well-maintained car that's getting a regular maintenance detail is much cheaper than a neglected car needing a deep restoration. Big difference in time and product usage.
Add-ons
Pet hair removal, headlight restoration, engine bay detailing, leather conditioning, paint correction, and ceramic coating are all priced separately.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Why does mobile detailing cost about the same as shop detailing?
Because pricing is based on time spent and supplies used, not on real estate. The mobile rig brings water, power, lighting, and equipment to you. You're paying for the convenience of not driving anywhere, not for a discounted service.
Is the cheapest detailing in Pittsburgh worth it?
Usually not. Sub-$100 'full details' typically involve a quick wash, basic vacuum, and a spray-on shine product. Reputable detailing involves hours of work and proper chemicals, which is hard to do at that price point.
How often should I get my car detailed?
A full detail twice a year (spring + fall) is the minimum for most drivers. Maintenance details monthly between full details keep the car looking sharp. Subscription plans make this convenient.
What's included in Bob's Detailing packages?
