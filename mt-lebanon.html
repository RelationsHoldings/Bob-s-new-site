Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you really come all the way to Mt. Lebanon?
Yes. Mt. Lebanon is one of our regularly-served areas. We're based in Cranberry Township and serve Mt. Lebanon multiple times per week. Same-week appointments are usually available.
Do you need access to my water or electric?
No. Our mobile detailing rig is fully self-contained. We bring our own water tank, generator, and lighting. You don't need to provide anything except a reasonably level place for us to set up.
Can you detail my car at my office or apartment?
Absolutely. We regularly work in office parking lots, apartment garages, and condo lots in Mt. Lebanon. Just confirm with your building that vendors are allowed, and we'll handle the rest.
How do I book?
The fastest way is to book online at our booking site or call/text us at (412) 925-8970. We'll confirm your address, vehicle, and preferred service, then send you a date.
Do you offer subscriptions or recurring details?
Yes. Our Clean Car Club offers monthly recurring details at a discounted rate. Visit our Subscriptions page or ask when you book. Fleet plans are also available for businesses with multiple vehicles.
What payment methods do you accept?
Card, cash, Venmo, and Zelle. Payment is collected after the service is complete and you've inspected the work.
