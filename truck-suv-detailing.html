Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you charge extra for trucks and large SUVs?
Yes. Fairly. Detailing a Suburban or F-250 takes 50-100% more time and product than detailing a Honda Civic. We quote pricing based on vehicle size and condition. No surprise fees.
Can you handle work trucks with serious dirt buildup?
Absolutely. We specialize in returning work trucks and contractor vehicles to clean condition. The dirtier they are, the more satisfying the before/after. Though deep cases may need a multi-stage clean.
Do you clean truck beds and bed liners?
Yes. We clean spray-in liners, rubber bed mats, and bare metal beds. Each gets the right product so we don't damage the surface. Plastic liners get scrubbed and dressed, metal gets washed and protected.
Can you detail a lifted truck or oversized SUV?
Yes. We come with platforms and step ladders to safely reach every panel on lifted trucks. No improvising or risking your property.
Will ceramic coating help on a work truck?
Strongly recommended. Trucks see more salt, mud, sun, and chemical exposure than any other vehicle. Ceramic coating dramatically slows down clear coat damage and makes cleaning easier between washes.
