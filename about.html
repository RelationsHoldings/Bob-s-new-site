About Bob's Detailing

Built on Pride. Driven by the Details.

Mobile car detailing for the Pittsburgh area — born from a decade of obsessing over the small stuff and built to make premium detailing feel effortless for the customer.

Our Story

A Decade of Detailing — Made Official in 2024

Bob's been detailing cars for over ten years. What started as a careful side hustle for friends, neighbors, and a steady stream of word-of-mouth clients turned into a reputation for getting things right that other shops rushed past. By 2024, the demand made the decision easy: time to make Bob's Detailing official, full-time, and mobile.

The mobile model wasn't a shortcut — it was the goal. Most of our clients lead full lives. Coordinating a half-day around dropping off and picking up a vehicle from a fixed-location shop is the part of car ownership nobody loves. So we built around that. Fully self-contained mobile rig, our own water and power, premium tools, and a pop-up workspace that lets us deliver shop-quality results in your driveway, your office parking lot, or your apartment garage.

What makes Bob's different isn't a single piece of equipment or a secret product. It's the volume of small decisions added up over a decade of real work — pH-balanced foam instead of dish soap, clay-bar decontamination before paint correction, hot-water extraction instead of surface vacuuming, paint-thickness gauges instead of guessing. Most of our customers don't see those details directly. They just see a car that looks better, smells fresher, and stays cleaner longer than they expected.

If you've worked with Bob before, you know. If you haven't yet — welcome. We'd love to be your detailer.
