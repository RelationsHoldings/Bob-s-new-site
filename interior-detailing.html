Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does an interior detail take?
A standard interior detail takes 2–4 hours. Deep cleans on heavily-used vehicles, vehicles with pet hair, or full extractions can take 4–6+ hours. We'll give you a precise estimate when you book.
Can you remove pet hair from carpets and seats?
Yes — and this is one of our specialties. We use specialty tools (rubber brushes, dedicated extraction nozzles) to lift hair that vacuums alone won't touch. See our dedicated pet hair removal service for details.
Will you clean leather seats?
Absolutely. We use leather-specific cleaners and conditioners that lift dirt without stripping the natural oils, then seal with a UV-protective conditioner so the leather stays soft and resists cracking.
Can you get rid of cigarette smoke smell?
We can dramatically reduce or eliminate most odors with deep extraction, ozone treatment, and headliner cleaning. Heavy long-term smoke saturation may require multiple sessions or interior part replacement, but we'll be honest with you about what's realistic.
Do I need to be home during the appointment?
Not necessarily — as long as the vehicle is accessible and we have a way to confirm completion. Many clients leave the car at home or work and we keep them updated by text.
