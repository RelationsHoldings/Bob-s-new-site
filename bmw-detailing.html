Pittsburgh Specific Considerations

BMW owners hold their cars to a higher standard, and we hold our work to the same standard. From the 3 Series to the M5 to the X7, every BMW we touch gets a tailored approach based on color, age, paint condition, and how the car is used.
Mineral White, Alpine White, and BMW Individual paint
Lighter BMW paints show swirl marks easily and need specific compound/pad combinations. Our multi-stage paint correction restores depth without damaging the paint.
Dakota, Nappa, and Merino leather
BMW's leather grades each require different care products. We use leather-specific cleaners and conditioners for the grade in your vehicle.
M-model carbon fiber & ceramic trim
M-series trim pieces need products that won't strip the finish. We work with safe-for-trim solutions.
Kidney grilles & shadow line trim
Detail-level work means cleaning every slat of the kidney grille and properly maintaining the gloss black shadow line trim without leaving residue.
Ceramic coating for paint protection
A ceramic coating dramatically protects BMW paint from salt, sap, and Pittsburgh winters.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you detail M-series and BMW Individual cars?
Absolutely. We've worked on M3s, M5s, X5 M, and BMW Individual cars. Higher-end BMWs get extra attention to detail. Kidney grille cleaning, shadow line maintenance, M-specific carbon fiber care.
Can you fix the swirls in my white BMW?
Yes. White BMW paint shows swirls particularly visibly. Our multi-stage paint correction removes swirls, RIDS, and oxidation safely. We measure paint thickness throughout to ensure we don't go too far.
Will detailing void my BMW warranty?
No. Mobile detailing doesn't affect your manufacturer warranty. Quite the opposite. Keeping your BMW well-maintained extends paint life and helps preserve resale value.
Do you have ceramic coating for BMW paint?
Yes. We use ceramic coatings rated for 2-5 years depending on tier. For Pittsburgh winters, this is one of the best investments you can make for protecting your BMW.
How long does a full BMW detail take?
A maintenance detail takes 2-3 hours. A full Signature package detail with deep interior shampoo and paint correction takes 4-6 hours. Multi-day ceramic coating jobs take 1-2 days.
