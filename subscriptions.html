Common Questions

Can I change my plan later?
Absolutely. Upgrade, downgrade, or switch frequency anytime. Changes take effect on your next billing cycle.
What if I need to skip a visit?
Life happens. Pause or reschedule with at least 48 hours notice, no penalty.
How does fleet billing work?
Fleet accounts receive a single consolidated invoice. Add or remove vehicles anytime. Minimum 5 vehicles to qualify.
Is there a contract?
No long-term contracts. All memberships are month-to-month. Cancel anytime with no penalties.
Can I add a second vehicle?
Yes! Add a 2nd vehicle to your membership and save 10% on both. Mix and match sizes and tiers.
Will my price ever go up?
Never. Your rate is locked in at sign-up. No price increases for as long as you're a member.
