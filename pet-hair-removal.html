Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does pet hair removal take?
Most pet hair removal jobs take 1.5–3 hours depending on hair volume and vehicle size. Heavy cases (long-haired dogs, multiple animals, years of buildup) can take longer.
Do you charge extra for pet hair?
Pet hair removal is included in our interior detail and full detail packages. For severely heavy cases, there may be a small surcharge. We'll let you know upfront when you book.
Will you damage the upholstery?
No. Our tools are designed to lift hair without damaging fabric, leather, or carpet fibers. We've done this thousands of times.
Can you also clean drool, accidents, or pet odors?
Yes. We can hot-water extract carpets and seats, treat odors with enzyme cleaners, and steam-sanitize to remove bacteria. If you have a specific situation, mention it when you book and we'll quote accordingly.
Do I need to vacuum first?
Nope. Just leave it as is. The more we have to work with, the more we can show you what we cleared.
